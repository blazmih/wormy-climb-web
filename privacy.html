Wormy Climb – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Wormy Climb ("the game") is made by blazmih. This page explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it to what the game needs to work.

What the game stores on your phone

Your progress (apples, skins, house, pets, scores, settings) is saved on your device.

Online features (Unity Gaming Services)

  • Guest account: when you start the game, an anonymous player account is created automatically. No email or real name is needed.
  • Leaderboard: the player name you choose, your country (if you pick one) and your best height are shown to other players on the world leaderboard.
  • Cloud save: a copy of your game progress is stored with your player account so you can get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling.

These services are provided by Unity Technologies. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game can show an ad only when you choose to watch one (for example to continue a climb or finish a daily mission). To show ads, Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and basic device information. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings.

Feedback and bug reports

If you use "Send feedback" or "Report a bug", your email app opens a message to us. It includes the game version, phone model, operating system, screen size and your best height so we can fix problems. We only use it to answer you and improve the game.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't collect your contacts, photos, precise location or messages.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes the data on your phone. To delete your online player data (leaderboard entry and cloud save), email us from the address below with your player name and we will delete it within 30 days.
